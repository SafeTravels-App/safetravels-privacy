Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 September 2026  ·  Last updated: 20 November 2026

SafeTravels is a travel risk intelligence application. This policy explains what personal data we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and what rights you have.

1 Data We Collect

1.1 Account data

Email address — collected when you create an account via Firebase Authentication. Used to authenticate you. If you send a safe check-in, your email address is stored with that event and included so your chosen emergency contacts can identify the sender.

1.2 Location data

Device location — accessed while the app is in use, with your permission, to identify your country of presence and display relevant information. The app may refresh location while active or when an active trip resumes. This routine lookup is not stored as location history. Typed place names may be sent to the Apple or Google geocoding service available on your device to obtain route coordinates. A safe check-in includes precise location only when you select that option for the check-in. The Firebase record stores latitude and longitude; the notification sends coordinates rounded to four decimal places (approximately 11 metres) to all saved emergency contacts, up to five. Permission can be withdrawn in device settings.

1.3 Traveler profile

  • Traveler type (e.g. Tourist, Business Traveler, Journalist) and home country — stored in your secure account record on Firebase. Traveler type may match a trip to an approved role-specific exposure finding; it is not used as a fixed score multiplier. Home country supports trip-planning and advisory context. When you open an emergency card, the app may send your two-letter home-country code and the destination's two-letter country code to a Firebase Cloud Function to look for a current, official mission record. The request does not include your name, email, identity-context fields or precise location.
  • Identity context — gender, LGBTQ+ status, and travel companions. Stored on your device only in local preferences. These fields are not written to Firebase Firestore, are not sent in the current POST-T or Trip Risk Assessment requests, and do not change the current assessment scores.

1.4 Trips and check-ins

  • Trip records — destination, date range, and any generated risk assessments, when generated after all evidence gates pass — stored in your secure account on Firebase, readable only by your authenticated account.
  • Safe check-in events — sender email, trip reference, destination label, optional latitude and longitude, delivery result and notification audit details are stored in your account and used to notify all emergency contacts saved in your account, up to five.

1.5 Emergency contacts

Names and contact details you voluntarily enter are protected by authenticated access controls and Firebase encryption at rest, and are used only to send safe check-in notifications.

1.6 Push notification tokens

Your device's Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token is stored in your account to deliver qualifying governed travel alerts. You can withdraw notification permission at any time in your device settings.

1.7 Subscription data

Subscription status and purchase identifiers are managed by RevenueCat (see §3). We do not store payment card details. After account deletion, SafeTravels uses a server-only write block keyed to the former Firebase account identifier for up to 24 hours so a still-valid session token cannot recreate account data. The temporary record is removed after cleanup completes. SafeTravels retains a separate one-way deletion-suppression token so a later store webhook cannot reattach a deleted account identifier.

1.8 App and account security

SafeTravels uses Firebase App Check to reject requests that do not come from an attested app instance. Android uses Google Play Integrity; Apple platforms use App Attest with DeviceCheck fallback. Google and Apple may process app-instance, device-integrity and attestation information to issue the token. SafeTravels does not store the raw attestation token. It stores short-lived, account-linked request counters containing the function name, UTC count buckets and expiry time to limit automated abuse. These counters are removed during account deletion.

3 Third Parties

Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Firebase App Check, and Firebase Cloud Messaging are provided by Google LLC. App Check uses Google Play Integrity on Android and Apple App Attest with DeviceCheck fallback on Apple platforms. Data is processed in Google-operated services.

AI-assisted guidance (OpenAI)

Where a destination category has no source-backed level, SafeTravels may ask OpenAI, L.L.C. (through the OpenAI API) to draft separately labelled AI-assisted guidance for that destination. The request contains only the destination and dated, sourced country evidence: official travel advice from Global Affairs Canada and the US Department of State, or Netherlands Ministry of Foreign Affairs advice where those two are silent. It contains no account identifier, name, email address, emergency contacts, trip details or device location. The guidance is generated per destination, stored by SafeTravels and shown to anyone who opens that destination; it is not about you. SafeTravels sends these requests with OpenAI’s response storage option turned off; OpenAI’s own handling of API data is governed by its policies. This feature runs only after its release checks pass; until then, categories without a level show what to check and official links.

Driving estimates (Google Maps Platform)

When a Pro user asks for a driving estimate, SafeTravels sends the coordinates of that trip’s saved route points and the departure time to the Google Routes API, and shows the distance and estimated time it returns, credited to Google Maps. No name, email address or account identifier is sent. SafeTravels does not store the distance, time or route: the result is shown on screen only. To apply usage limits it keeps only the number of driving checks each account made that day, with no route or location. Use of this feature is subject to the Google Maps Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

SafeTravels may also use OpenAI to translate the Netherlands Ministry of Foreign Affairs’ public travel advice from Dutch into English. Only the ministry’s published text is sent, never anything about you. Where an English version is shown, it is labelled as a machine translation, and the Dutch original stays one tap away.

Check-in email delivery (Hostinger; Twilio SendGrid as an alternative)

If you send a safe check-in, SafeTravels sends the email from safe@safetravelstrm.com through its Hostinger business email service (Hostinger International Ltd.) to all emergency contacts saved in your account, up to five. The email service receives recipient names and email addresses, your email (in the sender line, and as the Reply-To address when your email is verified), check-in time, trip location label and any coordinates you explicitly chose to include, solely to deliver the check-in email you asked to send. We may use Twilio SendGrid instead to deliver these emails; it then receives the same information for the same purpose. SafeTravels does not copy every check-in to an undisclosed central monitoring address.

4 Data Retention

  • Account data is retained while your account is active.
  • Identity context (gender, LGBTQ+, companions) is stored only on your device and is deleted when you delete the app or clear its data.
  • Trip assessments and check-in history are retained in your account until you delete the relevant trip or your account. Server-side deletion handlers remove assessment subrecords and current and legacy check-in records that the mobile client cannot delete directly.
  • When you delete your account (see §5), server-side cleanup begins with the deletion event. Firebase account content and account-identifying fields are permanently deleted or de-identified within 30 days. A server-only account write block may retain the former Firebase account identifier for up to 24 hours solely to prevent data recreation from a still-valid session token. Purchase providers may retain their own transaction records under their stated or legally required retention periods; SafeTravels may retain a de-identified receipt record (event and product identifiers, timestamps, status and payload digest) after its account identifiers are removed.

5 Your Rights and Account Deletion

  • 👁 Access your account data via the app's Profile screen.
  • ✏️ Correct your traveler profile at any time in the Profile screen.
  • 🗑 Delete your account in-app: Profile → Delete Account. This immediately deletes your Firebase Authentication record and starts server-side removal of Firestore data (profile, trips, contacts, check-ins and related operational records), which completes within 30 days. Local preferences are cleared on the device. RevenueCat and Apple or Google subscription records may be subject to their own retention schedules.
  • ✉️ Delete your account without the app: email safe@safetravelstrm.com from the email address registered to your SafeTravels account, with the subject “Delete account”. We delete the account in the same way as in-app deletion: your sign-in record, profile, trips, emergency contacts, check-ins and related operational records are removed, completing within 30 days. Only the limited records described in §4 remain (a de-identified purchase receipt record, and the purchase providers’ own records). If the request comes from another address, we will ask you to confirm it from the registered one. We will never ask for your password. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription: cancel it in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions) or on your Apple device (Settings → your name → Subscriptions).
  • 🔔 Withdraw location or notification permissions at any time in your device settings.
  • 📩 Request a data export or erasure by contacting us (see §7).

Delete selected data without deleting your account

  • In the app: delete a trip (My Trips → the trip → Delete trip); its assessments and the check-in records sent for it are then removed from our servers. Remove an emergency contact in Profile → Emergency Contacts. Clear the optional device-only context in Profile → Clear all exposure profile data; it is never stored on our servers.
  • By email: write to safe@safetravelstrm.com from the email address registered to your SafeTravels account, with the subject “Delete my data”, and say what you want deleted (for example a trip, check-in history or emergency contacts). We complete the request within 30 days. If the request comes from another address, we will ask you to confirm it from the registered one.
  • What stays while you keep the account: your sign-in email, account record and plan status are needed to run the account and can be removed only by deleting the account (see above). Purchase records are kept as described in §4.
  • We will never ask for your password.
If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA) or United Kingdom, you have rights under GDPR / UK GDPR — including the right to object, restrict processing, and lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority.

6 Security

All data in transit is encrypted using TLS (HTTPS). Firebase Firestore data at rest is encrypted by Google. Access to account records is enforced by security rules; server-generated assessment records are readable only by the trip owner and writable only by the backend. Identity context fields are not written to Firebase.
