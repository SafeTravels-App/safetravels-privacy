2. Description of Service

SafeTravels presents official travel advice from Global Affairs Canada and the US Department of State, with its dates and sources. For each destination it shows separate Political, Operational, Security, Terrorism and Travel categories, each with a level from 0 to 4 only where published statistics or official statements support one. These are ordinal classifications, not incident probabilities, and are not averaged into a composite score. Political and Operational levels use the World Bank's Worldwide Governance Indicators (aggregate estimates), which are perception-based; the year and margin of error are shown. A category without a source-backed level has no level and never means low risk. Beside such a category, SafeTravels may show separately labelled AI-assisted content (“AI-assisted guidance — not official advice, not a SafeTravels score”, or “Provisional AI estimate — not a validated score”), only when it passes its own release gates and cites the sources it draws on; it does not replace the insufficient status and is not a validated score. Pro trip context shows which official regional warnings name the places in a saved trip; it is not a trip score, and a place not named in a warning is not confirmed safe.

SafeTravels presents facts and methodology-derived assessments for the user to evaluate. It is not a security manager and does not make, approve, or prohibit a travel decision.

SafeTravels does not constitute official government travel advice. Always verify travel information with your government's foreign ministry before travelling.

SafeTravels is not an emergency or assistance service. It does not monitor trips or safe check-ins, contact emergency services or responders, or arrange evacuation, medical, security or consular help. A safe check-in is an email to the contacts you choose; SafeTravels does not watch for it or act on it. In an emergency, call the local emergency number or your government's consular emergency line.
